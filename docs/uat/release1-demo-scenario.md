# Release 1 UAT Demonstration Scenario

All records used in this scenario are fictional.

1. Sign in as an authorised complaint intake officer and register a DEMO administrative complaint.
2. Perform intake and jurisdiction screening; show that decline, defer, refer and accept paths require a recorded reason.
3. Accept the matter, create an investigation plan and register a DEMO evidence item with custody metadata.
4. Demonstrate draft findings followed by Right to be Heard before final assessment.
5. Open a restricted DEMO Leadership matter and show that a user without `LEADERSHIP_RESTRICTED` cannot read it even with an administrative role.
6. Open a DEMO Annual Statement, demonstrate comparative review, and show a variance flag labelled as an analytical prompt rather than a misconduct finding.
7. Record a DEMO recommendation, agency response, corrective action evidence and compliance review.
8. Prepare a DEMO Members of Commission submission and demonstrate that the system records—but does not invent—the Commission decision.
9. Review audit events and an authorised export event.
10. Sign out and verify protected routes require authentication when Supabase is configured.
